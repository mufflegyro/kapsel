Yummle v1.3.5 is a small reliability release with one scheduling fix: downloads of scheduled YouTube premieres no longer burn their retry attempts every ten minutes while the video is still unreleased. No feature changes and no data migrations.

## Fixed

- **Scheduled premieres no longer fail their way out of the queue.** When yt-dlp hits a video that is a scheduled premiere (`ERROR: [youtube] <id>: Premieres in 26 hours`), Kapsel treated it as a generic failure and retried every 10 minutes. Because download jobs allow 3 attempts, any premiere more than ~20 minutes away exhausted all attempts and was permanently failed before the video ever published. The retry scheduler now parses the stated time until the premiere from yt-dlp's output and schedules the retry for just after the premiere starts (stated duration + 30 minutes), so the download lands shortly after the video is published. Multi-part durations are supported ("1 hour 30 minutes", "3 days"); members-only videos are still marked and skipped terminally as before, and auth challenges keep their 1-hour retry delay.

## Notes

- **The retry waits for the premiere, not the clock.** A job failing with "Premieres in X" sleeps until the stated duration plus a 30-minute buffer; the effective minimum is the 30-minute buffer (relevant for premieres only minutes away). If the stated duration cannot be parsed, the job falls back to the existing 10-minute retry, so nothing gets stuck.
- **Non-premiere behavior is unchanged.** Generic failures still retry after 10 minutes, bot/age challenges after 1 hour, and members-only videos are still skipped terminally.

No upgrade steps: nothing to migrate, nothing to configure, and no data is rewritten.
