Yummle v1.3.0 turns the search results page into a video-first view: episodes lead, channels get their own lane, and every tile tells you why it matched.

## Added

- **Episode-first search results.** Searching the archive now leads with the videos themselves. Episode tiles — thumbnail, duration badge, title, and channel — fill the page in a grid that mirrors the library, while channel and playlist matches move into a compact "Channels & playlists" block above it. A query that names a channel still reaches the channel page in one click from that block, and a query that only matches channels or playlists shows the block plus a "No matching episodes in the archive yet." note instead of a channel-led list.
- **"Matched in" context on tiles.** A video can match through its title, description, subtitles, or viewer comments. Title matches stay clean; anything else carries a small "Matched in description / subtitles / comments" tag so you always know why a tile appeared.
- **A results summary line.** Under the "Search Results" heading, a subtitle reads "N results for "…"". It counts unique videos, channels, and playlists rather than raw match rows, so a video that matched three ways still counts once, and it is announced politely to screen readers when it updates.

## Changed

- **Episodes are ordered newest-first.** The tile grid sorts by publish date, descending, so the freshest uploads lead the page regardless of where the search engine ranked them. Videos without a publish date sort last. Channel and playlist matches keep their relevance ordering.
- **One tile per video.** A video that matches on several fields at once — title, description, subtitles, and comments — now appears once, represented by its best-ranked match, instead of once per matching field.

No schema changes and no API changes; upgrading is a drop-in binary swap.
