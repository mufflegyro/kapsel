Yummle v1.1.0 turns media retention into a documented contract — and extends it to everything you have finished watching.

## Added

- **Watched media is cleaned up, whatever its origin.** Retention previously only ever removed channel auto-downloads: a manually downloaded or imported video kept its media file forever, even after being watched. A video marked watched — via the watch toggle or by watched playback progress — now has its media removed at the next daily retention run once a 24-hour grace period passes. Only the media file goes; the video record (metadata, watch state) stays and the media can be re-downloaded. "Keep forever" protects a video absolutely, and the timer restarts on any watch-progress write, so a video being re-watched is not deleted mid-playback.
- **The watched grace is configurable.** `KAPSEL_RETENTION_WATCHED_AFTER` (default `24h`) controls how long watched media lingers before cleanup. Set `0s` to disable watched-media cleanup entirely and keep the pre-1.1.0 behavior.
- **The retention contract is documented.** A new "Media Retention" section in [docs/deployment.md](https://github.com/mufflegyro/yummle/blob/main/docs/deployment.md) spells out both cleanup rules — watched media of any origin, and stale unstarted channel auto-downloads beyond the newest 2 per channel — including the deliberate asymmetry: started-but-never-finished media and unwatched manual or imported downloads stay until they are watched or marked Keep forever.

No schema changes; upgrading is a drop-in binary swap.
